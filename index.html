About

Engineering clarity from concept to implementation.

I am an engineering professional focused on turning complex requirements, interfaces, and stakeholder needs into practical transportation solutions.

My background spans passenger rail vehicle development, interior systems integration, design coordination, supplier management, and engineering change. In design leadership roles, I bring cross-functional teams together, surface technical risk, and help move decisions forward with clear analysis and communication.

I approach engineering with a continuous-improvement mindset. Alongside robust technical fundamentals, I use digital tools, automation, and AI-assisted workflows to improve traceability, reduce repetitive work, and give teams more time for high-value problem solving.
